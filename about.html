A Message from Our Founder & Director

Dr. Venkata Bharadwaj

Consultant ENT, Head and Neck Surgeon (Director)

  • Qualifications: MBBS, MS (ENT, HEAD AND NECK SURGERY)
  • Experience: 11 Years

"The launch of Sri Sitara Hospitals was born from a profound commitment to elevate healthcare standards in Karimnagar. Having spent over a decade practicing specialized ENT, Head and Neck Surgery, I realized the immense need for a multi-speciality center that blends advanced surgical precision with foundational medical care—all delivered with the highest degree of ethical transparency and human warmth.

My team and I are 'Inspired to Serve,' dedicating ourselves to providing comprehensive care, from complex head and neck procedures to critical internal medicine management. We utilize cutting-edge technology and evidence-based protocols to ensure safety and superior clinical outcomes for every patient. We promise to be your trusted partner in health."

Our Core Vision

Inspired to Serve.

Since our inception, Sri Sitara Hospitals has been driven by a singular mission: to make world-class medical expertise and technology accessible to the community in Karimnagar. Our commitment is rooted in trust, ethical practice, and genuine compassion. We are built to deliver superior health outcomes.
