Surgical Oncology

Specialized surgical expertise for the diagnosis and curative treatment of various cancers.

Precision Surgery in Comprehensive Cancer Care

Our Surgical Oncology department, led by Consultant Surgical Oncologist **Dr. Prashanth Kumar** (MS, MCh Surgical Oncology), provides definitive surgical intervention as a core component of cancer treatment. We work closely with other specialists (medical oncology and radiation oncology) to provide personalized, multi-modality care plans.

Focus Areas & Surgical Expertise

Dr. Prashanth Kumar specializes in highly complex surgical procedures aimed at achieving optimal oncological outcomes, including:

Breast Oncology:

  • Breast Conservation Surgery (BCS)
  • Modified Radical Mastectomy (MRM)
  • Sentinel Lymph Node Biopsy

Gastrointestinal (GI) Oncology:

  • Colorectal Cancer Surgery
  • Stomach and Esophageal Cancer Surgery
  • Liver and Pancreatic Tumor Resections

Other Oncological Surgeries

We treat a variety of other solid tumors, ensuring the widest margin of excision possible while preserving function and quality of life.

  • **Head & Neck Oncology:** Management of oral, thyroid, and laryngeal cancers.
  • **Gynecological Oncology:** Surgical treatment for ovarian, uterine, and cervical cancers.
  • **Uro-Oncology:** Surgery for kidney, bladder, and prostate cancers.
  • **Soft Tissue Sarcomas:** Wide excision and limb salvage procedures.
