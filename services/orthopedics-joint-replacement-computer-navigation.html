Orthopedics & Joint Replacement

Advanced solutions for joint pain, injuries, and mobility restoration, powered by cutting-edge Computer Navigation technology.

Excellence in Orthopedic Care

The Centre for Orthopedics at Sri Sitara Hospitals is dedicated to relieving pain and restoring active lifestyles. We provide comprehensive care for a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions, from common fractures to complex joint disorders. Our approach combines the expertise of our specialists with state-of-the-art diagnostic and surgical technology to ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients.

Conditions We Treat

  • Arthritis & Joint Pain
  • Complex Trauma & Fractures
  • Sports Injuries
  • Spine Disorders
  • Ligament & Tendon Injuries
  • Degenerative Joint Disease

Advanced Computer Navigation for Joint Replacement

We are proud to be one of the few centers in Karimnagar offering Computer-Navigated Joint Replacement Surgery. This advanced technology acts like a GPS for surgeons, allowing for unparalleled precision in implant placement. The benefits include smaller incisions, faster recovery, reduced pain, and significantly longer-lasting joint replacements.
