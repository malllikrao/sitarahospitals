General Medicine & Diabetology

Comprehensive care for acute illnesses, chronic conditions, and expert management of **Diabetes and lifestyle diseases**.

Your First Step to Internal Health

The Department of **General Medicine** serves as the central point for comprehensive patient care, diagnosis, and treatment. Our specialists manage a vast spectrum of non-surgical health issues, providing both primary care for common ailments and expert management for complex, multi-system diseases. We focus on diagnosing difficult conditions and coordinating care across various specialties.

Core Services: General Medicine

  • Hypertension and Cardiac Risk Management
  • Infectious Disease Diagnosis and Treatment
  • Preventive Health Checkups
  • Thyroid and other Endocrine Disorders
  • Treatment of Geriatric and Chronic illnesses
  • Undiagnosed Illnesses Management

Specialized Diabetology Care

Sitara Hospitals offers a focused approach to Diabetes Mellitus, a rapidly growing health challenge. Our **Diabetology** team provides personalized treatment plans aimed at optimal blood sugar control, prevention of long-term complications, and patient education.

Diabetes Management Focus

  • Intensive Insulin Therapy and Advanced Medication Management.
  • Diabetic Foot Care and Retinopathy Screening.
  • Nutritional Counseling and Lifestyle Modification.
  • Management of Gestational Diabetes.
