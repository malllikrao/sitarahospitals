Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery

Expert surgical solutions for restoring form and function after trauma, and enhancing natural aesthetics.

Restoring Lives through Reconstruction and Aesthetic Surgery

Our Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery department, led by **Dr. Ashok Reddy** (MBBS, MS, MCh Plastic Surgery), is dedicated to treating defects and deformities resulting from birth, trauma, burns, and disease. We prioritize functional recovery while achieving the best possible cosmetic outcome.

Core Reconstructive Services

Reconstruction is the foundation of our specialty, offering hope and healing after major injuries or cancer surgery.

  • **Trauma Reconstruction:** Repair and coverage of complex defects involving soft tissue, bone, and nerves (e.g., hand injuries, open fractures).
  • **Burn Care & Post-Burn Deformity Correction:** Comprehensive management of acute burns and subsequent contracture release and scarring correction.
  • **Flap and Graft Surgeries:** Utilizing advanced micro-surgical techniques for tissue transfer to complex wounds.
  • **Congenital Deformity Correction:** Treatment of defects present at birth, suchs as Cleft Lip and Palate.

Aesthetic & Cosmetic Procedures

We also offer a range of cosmetic procedures aimed at enhancing appearance and self-confidence, all performed with the highest standards of safety and artistry.

Popular Aesthetic Treatments:

  • Facial rejuvenation (e.g., Rhinoplasty, Blepharoplasty)
  • Body contouring (e.g., Liposuction, Abdominoplasty)
  • Breast Surgery (e.g., Reduction, Augmentation)
  • Scar revision and removal of benign lesions
