Maxillofacial Surgery

Specialized surgical care for complex conditions of the face, jaw, mouth, and neck.

Reconstruction, Trauma, and Oral Pathologies

Our **Maxillofacial Surgery** department, led by Consultant Surgeon **Dr. R. Nishanth** (MDS, Maxillofacial Surgeon), provides surgical expertise for a range of conditions affecting the mouth, jaws, facial bones, and soft tissues. We focus on restoring function and aesthetic appearance after trauma, disease, or birth defects.

Facial Trauma and Fractures

We offer immediate and definitive surgical treatment for facial injuries resulting from accidents, including:

  • Facial bone fractures (**Zygoma, Mandible, Maxilla**)
  • Orbital and Nasal bone reconstruction
  • Soft tissue injuries and lacerations

Oral Pathology and Correction

Dr. Nishanth specializes in the diagnosis and surgical removal of cysts, tumors, and correcting dental-skeletal discrepancies.

Key Maxillofacial Procedures:

  • Surgical correction of Jaw Deformities
  • TMJ (Temporomandibular Joint) Surgery
  • Cyst and Tumor Removal from Jaws
  • Complex Wisdom Tooth & Impaction Surgery
  • Dental Implant Placement (Advanced Cases)

We collaborate closely with the ENT and Plastic Surgery departments for comprehensive reconstructive solutions, especially for head and neck defects.
