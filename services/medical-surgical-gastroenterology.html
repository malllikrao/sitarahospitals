Medical & Surgical Gastroenterology

Advanced care for all diseases of the digestive system, liver, pancreas, and biliary tract.

Expertise in Digestive Health and Liver Care

The **Gastroenterology Department** at Sitara Hospitals provides comprehensive, state-of-the-art management for disorders of the esophagus, stomach, small intestine, colon, liver, gallbladder, and pancreas. We offer both advanced **Medical Gastroenterology** for non-surgical treatment and **Surgical Gastroenterology** (performed by our General Surgery team) for conditions requiring intervention.

Medical Gastroenterology & Endoscopy

Our medical gastroenterologist specializes in diagnosis and therapeutic procedures using advanced endoscopy equipment.

Diagnostic Procedures:

  • Diagnostic **Upper GI Endoscopy**
  • Diagnostic **Colonoscopy**
  • Liver Biopsy

Treatments Handled:

  • Management of Liver Diseases (Hepatitis, Cirrhosis)
  • IBS, IBD (Crohn's Disease, Ulcerative Colitis)
  • Therapeutic Endoscopy (Polypectomy, Variceal Banding)

Surgical Management (In collaboration with General Surgery)

Conditions requiring surgical correction, such as gallstones and certain cancers, are managed jointly for seamless care.

Key Surgical Procedures:

  • Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder removal for stones).
  • Complex Hernia Repairs.
  • Surgery for Gastrointestinal Tumors.
