Emergency Hotline: 8930 104 108

The Lifeline of Sitara Hospitals: Our ICU & Trauma Unit

Our Emergency and Critical Care unit is the **highest priority** department at Sitara Hospitals. We operate a dedicated, fully-staffed service **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, ensuring immediate attention for all medical emergencies, accidents, and life-threatening conditions. Our team, comprising specialists in Critical Care Medicine, Anaesthesiology, and Emergency Surgery, is trained for rapid assessment and stabilization.

Critical Care Unit (ICU) Capabilities

The Sitara ICU is equipped with advanced technology to manage the most critically ill patients:

  • Advanced Hemodynamic Monitoring
  • Continuous Cardiac Monitoring
  • Mechanical Ventilation and Life Support
  • 24/7 Access to Diagnostic Imaging (C-ARM)
  • Dedicated Critical Care Physicians
  • Poisoning and Overdose Management

Emergency Services Provided

From minor injuries to major life-saving interventions, our trauma team is prepared for:

  • Trauma and Accident Care
  • Stroke and Head Injury Management
  • Acute Cardiac Events (Heart Attacks)
  • Emergency General Surgery

❗ When to Call the Emergency Line

If you or someone else experiences chest pain, severe bleeding, difficulty breathing, sudden paralysis, unconsciousness, or major trauma, dial **8930 104 108** immediately.
