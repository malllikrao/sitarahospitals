Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Complete healthcare dedicated to women's health, from adolescence through post-menopause, including high-risk maternity care.

A Centre for Comprehensive Women's Wellness

The Department of **Gynaecology and Obstetrics** offers specialized, compassionate care for women at every stage of life. Our services cover everything from routine health checkups and contraceptive advice to advanced surgical procedures and managing high-risk pregnancies, ensuring both mother and baby receive the best possible care.

Obstetrics (Maternity Care)

We provide continuous care throughout pregnancy, labour, and delivery, supported by experienced neonatologists and a fully equipped Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) readiness.

  • Antenatal, Perinatal, and Postnatal Care.
  • Management of high-risk pregnancies (e.g., preeclampsia, gestational diabetes).
  • Painless Labour options.
  • Normal Delivery and Caesarean Section (C-Section).

Gynaecology (Women's Health)

Our team addresses all aspects of gynaecological health, focusing on preventive care and effective treatment of conditions affecting the female reproductive system.

Conditions Treated:

  • Menstrual Irregularities and PCOD/PCOS
  • Uterine Fibroids, Ovarian Cysts, and Endometriosis
  • Infertility Evaluation and Basic Management
  • Menopause management and hormone therapy

Surgical Services:

  • Hysterectomy (Abdominal and Vaginal)
  • Laparoscopic Gynaecological Surgery
  • Family Planning and Contraception
  • Cancer Screening (Pap smear) and Colposcopy
