Pulmonology

Specialized medical care for all disorders of the lungs and respiratory system, ensuring you breathe easy.

Advanced Respiratory Diagnosis and Management

The Pulmonology department, guided by **Dr. G. Srinivas** (MD, DNB Pulmonology), provides expert diagnosis and management for a wide range of acute and chronic respiratory illnesses. We focus on enhancing lung function and improving the quality of life for patients with breathing difficulties.

Chronic Lung Disease Management

Effective, long-term management is crucial for chronic conditions. Our programs focus on education, medication management, and lifestyle modification.

  • **COPD** (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease) and Emphysema management.
  • **Bronchial Asthma:** Personalized treatment plans, inhaler technique training, and trigger identification.
  • **Tuberculosis (TB):** Diagnosis and complete course management for all forms of TB.
  • **Interstitial Lung Diseases (ILD):** Diagnosis and specialized care for complex fibrotic lung conditions.

Interventional Pulmonology & Diagnostics

We use advanced diagnostic tools to investigate the cause of respiratory symptoms and perform minimally invasive therapeutic procedures.

Key Pulmonary Services:

  • **Spirometry/PFT** (Pulmonary Function Testing)
  • **Bronchoscopy:** Diagnostic and therapeutic procedures.
  • Pleural Fluid Aspiration/Biopsy
  • **Sleep Studies** (Polysomnography) for Sleep Apnea

Our team also manages acute respiratory failure, pneumonia, and provides critical care support including mechanical ventilation (NIV and invasive).
